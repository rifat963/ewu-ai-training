# Demo dataset: Career anxiety in the age of AI

3,156 university students in Bangladesh, 13 variables (age, gender, academic year, AI knowledge, AI tools used, career path, perceptions of AI, career anxiety, consent).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `dataset.xlsx` | Original survey file as published. **Start here.** |
| `dataset_variable_encoded.xlsx` | Numerically encoded version (contains one invalid code, `h`, to find in step 2) |
| `data_dictionary.csv` | Variable, type, unique values, missing values |

## Citation (required)

Islam, S., Piyas, B. R. C., Tisha, F. J., Nayem, H. K., Rahman, S., Preenon, B. R. C., & Hossen, S. (2026). Career anxiety in the age of artificial intelligence: Survey data of university students in Bangladesh. *Data in Brief, 67*, 112924. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2026.112924
